## About Me

I am a third-year Ph.D. student at Mathematical Department of LMU Munich (Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München) and the Huawei Research Center Munich, 2012 Laboratory, under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Gitta Kutyniok. My research focuses on World Model, Embodied AI, and Multi-Modal Diffusion Models.

Before beginning Ph.D., I earned my Master’s degree in Scientific Computing from the Technical University of Berlin (TUB) and ETH Zurich, where I worked in the Scalable Parallel Computing Laboratory (SPCL) and graduated with high distinction. I received my Bachelor’s degree in Safety Engineering from the University of Science and Technology Beijing (USTB).

## Research Interests

- **Computer Vision:** Controllable Video Generation, World Model, Multi-Modal Diffusion Model
- **Emboddied AI:** Robotic Policy learning, Vision-Language-Action model (VLA)
